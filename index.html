Installed Software Report

Takes about 2 minutes. Pick your system, follow the steps, then upload the PDF. The script only reads your list of installed software; it doesn’t install or change anything.

1

Open PowerShell

Press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter.

Open it normally. Do not choose “Run as administrator”; it isn’t needed.
2

Copy, paste, run

Click the button, click inside the PowerShell window, paste with Ctrl+V (or right-click) and press Enter. It’s one long line; that’s normal. Wait 10–20 seconds.

The script is saved in your Downloads folder, and the PDF installed_software.pdf is created in that same folder and opens automatically.

3

Upload the PDF

Open the form and upload installed_software.pdf from your Downloads folder.

Open the upload form
The PDF didn’t open, or only an .html file appeared?

Your PC has no Edge or Chrome available for PDF creation. Open the .html file that appeared in Downloads, press Ctrl+P, choose Save as PDF, then upload that.
